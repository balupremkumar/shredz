Shredz v2 mock

Static mock, not the app. Light screens first, then the same Diary and Dictation in dark (the app follows the iPhone setting). Yellow tags explain the fixes for the bugs you hit.

What changes from v0.2

  • Apple Health layout: inset grouped lists, one font size per job, 16 px side margin everywhere, every row 52 px or taller so fingers hit.
  • The ring is the hero: calories left in the middle, protein, carbs and fat as three thin bars beside it.
  • The big mic floats above the tab bar on Diary, bottom right in the thumb zone, labelled "Speak". One tap starts listening; it never needs the keyboard.
  • Every sheet sizes itself to the space above the keyboard, keeps its Save button docked right above the keyboard, and scrolls its own list. The page behind never scrolls.
  • Fixing an item is one tap on the row: a quantity sheet with a big number, the number keypad and the new calories. Swipe left still deletes.
  • Goal modes go: goals are grams per macro, calories worked out. The weekly report moves to one "Averages" row at the bottom of Weight.
  • Kove mark plus "Shredz" in the Kove typeface in the header; the Kove mark is the home-screen icon.

States covered per screen

Diary: empty day (shown), full day (shown), first open (welcome). Dictation: listening (shown), not recognised word (shown, red with "Add"), mic blocked or unsupported (falls back to the keyboard box with the keyboard mic hint), nothing heard ("Didn't catch that, try again"). Add food: results (shown), no match ("No food called 'banan', add it?"). Weight: no entries yet ("Log your first morning weight"). Loading is not shown: everything is on the phone and opens instantly.

Deliberate exceptions to your ban list

Rounded corners on grouped lists, sheets and the round mic: this is the iOS look you picked; no gradients, glass, glow, pills, chips or outline icons. Body text is the iPhone system font (SF) so it feels native; Schibsted Grotesk is used only for the Shredz wordmark.
