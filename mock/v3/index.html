Shredz v3, the design

Seven screens at phone size. One Log button in the middle of the tab bar opens one sheet, where you either speak or pick. Every number is entered on Shredz's own keypad, so the iPhone keyboard and its pop-up bar only ever appear for a food's name or a search. The keypad on screen 3 works, try it. The band behind the clock is part of the app frame: lists scroll beneath it and never over it.

5. Food editor

This is the screen that threw the pop-up bar: eight text boxes and a dropdown in one form. Now the numbers are rows on the keypad with our own Next, the unit is four buttons, and only the two name rows open the iPhone keyboard, one at a time.

6. Weigh-in

Two taps on most mornings: open, then Save, because it starts on yesterday's weight. A tenth up or down is one more tap. Or say "weight 82.4" in the Log sheet.
